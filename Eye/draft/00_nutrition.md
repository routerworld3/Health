For age-related nearsightedness (presbyopia) and overall eye health, certain natural ingredients have shown potential benefits:

1. Lutein and Zeaxanthin
These are carotenoids found in high concentrations in eye tissue.
Benefits:
Supplementation with lutein and zeaxanthin has been shown to slow the progression of age-related macular degeneration (AMD), a common aging-related eye condition. One large clinical trial found they slowed AMD progression by 9%.Don’t overlook lutein and zeaxanthin
Meta-analyses indicate benefits for eyesight in people with AMD.
In teenagers, these compounds have been shown to reduce the development of nearsightedness (myopia), though evidence for this effect in adults is limited so far.
2. Berry Extracts (Anthocyanins)
Recent research, such as a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial, suggests standardized berry extracts may improve certain visual function outcomes in people experiencing presbyopia (age-related nearsightedness).Eyes on the berry: A closer look at berry extract for eye health
3. Other Antioxidants and Nutrients
Antioxidants such as vitamin A, vitamin C, vitamin E, zinc, selenium, and omega-3 fatty acids may help reduce oxidative stress in eye tissues and are believed to play a protective role against various eye conditions, especially those associated with aging.Which supplements are of most interest for eyes and vision?
These nutrients can be found in diet or supplements and may contribute to maintaining eye health.
Summary:
Lutein and zeaxanthin are the most researched natural ingredients for supporting eye health and reducing the progression of age-related macular degeneration. Berry extracts (rich in anthocyanins) also show promise for improving visual function in presbyopia. Other antioxidants like vitamins A, C, E, zinc, selenium, and omega-3s support general eye health by reducing oxidative stress.
