Several natural ingredients and supplements have shown potential benefits for reducing sciatica-related pain based on recent studies and reviews:

1. Topical Henna Oil (Lawsonia inermis L.)
Study Details: A randomized double-blind clinical trial evaluated the effect of topical henna oil on pain intensity in patients with chronic sciatica.
Results: After 4 weeks, people using henna oil had reduced pain and improved disability scores compared to those using sesame or paraffin oil.
Topical henna oil for sciatica
2. Supplement Combination: Alpha Lipoic Acid (ALA), Acetyl-L-Carnitine, Resveratrol, and Vitamin D
Study Details: Young people with discogenic sciatica received a combination of ALA, acetyl-L-carnitine, resveratrol, and cholecalciferol (vitamin D), along with rehabilitation.
Results: Pain and disability decreased most in the combination group over 90 days, and quality of life improved more compared to supplement-only or rehab-only groups.

Additional Considerations
Some supplements commonly studied for back pain (but not specific to sciatica) include vitamin D, amino acids, vitamin B12 , alkaline minerals, collagen peptides (with vitamin C ), and various herbal remedies.
